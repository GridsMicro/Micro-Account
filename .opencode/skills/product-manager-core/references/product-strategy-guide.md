- **Goal:** Reduce FX volatility impact → lock in margin via pricing model
- **Actions:**
  - Pricing Model: 25% markup on USD (current decision as of 2026-09-21)
  - Diversify customer base: Find 1-2 additional GWS customers ($5-10K combined/month)

### Priority 2: Develop Professional Services Line
- **Goal:** Create scalable, high-margin service offerings beyond resale
- **Potential Products:**
  - AI transformation consulting (ChatGPT, Claude, automation)
  - Digital workplace optimization (GWS setup, governance, security)
  - Custom integration services (Zapier, Make, MCP-based solutions)
- **Actions:**
  - Interview 3-5 current resale customers about pain points
  - Define service packages (Discovery → Implementation → Support)
  - Price using value-based model (not hourly)
- **Success Metric:** 1 pilot engagement by Q2 2027

### Priority 3: Build Product-Led Growth with Micro-Account App
- **Goal:** Use Micro-Account as proof-of-concept for automation product
- **Vision:** Sell "Micro-Account for SMEs" as SaaS (accounting + tax automation)
- **Current State:** Internal tool for MICROTRONIC books
- **Actions:**
  - Extract core accounting features into reusable template
  - Add multi-company support
  - Build Thai tax automation (PP.30, PP.36, PND53 auto-filing)
  - Create landing page + pricing tiers

### Priority 4: Explore New Channels & Markets

## Pricing Strategy

### Current Model (GWS Resale)
- **Cost:** USD/month @ current THB exchange rate
- **Margin:** 25% markup on cost (effective Sep 2026)
- **Price to Customer:** 1.25 × (USD cost @ Thai bath rate on invoice date)
- **Rationale:** Absorb FX risk while maintaining predictable margin %

### Future Model (Services + SaaS)
- **Tiered SaaS Pricing:**
  - **Starter:** 500 THB/month (1 user, 10 invoices/month) — for freelancers
  - **Pro:** 1,500 THB/month (3 users, 100 invoices/month, tax reports) — SME sweet spot
  - **Enterprise:** Custom pricing (multi-company, advanced integrations, support)

- **Professional Services:**
  - **Discovery:** 5,000-10,000 THB (value-based, project scope)
  - **Implementation:** 10,000-50,000 THB (fixed deliverables)
  - **Support:** 1,000 THB/month (retainer + hourly for overages)

## Competitive Analysis

### Direct Competitors (Cloud Accounting SaaS)
- **Zoho Books:** Popular in Thailand, 90-300 THB/month, multi-currency, tax export
- **Wave:** Free in some regions, limited Thai tax support
- **FastBill:** EU-focused, not localized for Thailand

### Indirect Competitors (GWS Resale)
- **Google Cloud Partners:** Noventiq, CDW, TechData, Ingram Micro
- **Local SIs:** Synergy, Truevision, NEC, SOTACOM

### Microtronic's Advantage
- **Deep Thai tax knowledge** (PP.30, PP.36, PND53, WHT) — rare in SaaS market
- **Hands-on support** (not just software)
- **Google-native** (uses GWS for internal operations, understands ecosystem)
