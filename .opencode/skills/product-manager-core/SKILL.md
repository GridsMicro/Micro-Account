## Core Product Management Skills

### 1. **Product Spec Writer (PRD Generator)**
   - **Purpose:** Transform product ideas into complete PRD (Product Requirements Document)
   - **Use When:** Starting new feature, planning major release, clarifying requirements with team
   - **Outputs:**
     - Business case & success metrics
     - User personas & jobs-to-be-done
     - Feature breakdown (MoSCoW prioritization: Must, Should, Could, Won't)
     - User stories with acceptance criteria
     - Design/technical requirements
     - Launch plan & dependencies
   - **Template:** 1-line idea → Full PRD with user stories, acceptance criteria, risk analysis

### 2. **Story Map Builder (Backlog Visualization)**
   - **Purpose:** Organize requirements visually from Epic → Feature → Story structure
   - **Use When:** Planning release, communicating roadmap to stakeholders, identifying gaps
   - **Outputs:**
     - Interactive HTML story map
     - MoSCoW priority tagging
     - Release swimlane grouping
     - Dependency visualization
   - **Benefits:** Reduces miscommunication, makes priorities visible, helps team alignment

### 3. **Pricing Strategy Designer**
   - **Purpose:** Design, optimize, and communicate SaaS/product pricing models
   - **Use When:** Building pricing from scratch, redesigning existing pricing, planning price increase
   - **Key Tasks:**
     - Tier structure design (Basic, Pro, Enterprise)
     - Value metric selection (per-user, per-feature, usage-based)
     - Pricing page copywriting
     - Price elasticity analysis
     - Competitor benchmarking
     - Launch strategy (early adopter pricing, grandfather clauses, etc.)

### 4. **Gantt Planner (Project Timeline & CPM)**
   - **Purpose:** Generate interactive Gantt charts with Critical Path Method (CPM) analysis
   - **Use When:** Planning complex projects, identifying critical path, resource allocation, timeline estimation
   - **Outputs:**
     - Visual Gantt chart (HTML)
     - Critical path highlighted
     - Task dependencies & float times
     - Milestone tracking
     - Resource constraints

### 5. **Campaign Plan Generator**
   - **Purpose:** Create full campaign brief from objective to execution
   - **Use When:** Planning product launch, lead-gen push, awareness campaign, new market entry
   - **Outputs:**
     - Campaign objective & success metrics
     - Target audience definition
     - Messaging framework
     - Channel strategy (paid, organic, direct)
     - Weekly content calendar with dependencies
     - Budget & ROI projection
   - **Example Use:** Planning Google Workspace reseller campaign for Enterprise customers

### 6. **OKR Strategist (Goal Setting & Tracking)**
   - **Purpose:** Draft, break down, and review OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)
   - **Use When:** Setting quarterly goals, cascading team OKRs, conducting retrospectives
   - **Key Tasks:**
     - High-level objective writing (qualitative, inspiring)
     - Key results definition (measurable, time-bound)
     - OKR breakdown (company → team → individual)
     - Retrospective scoring & learning capture
   - **Framework:** 3-5 company OKRs → 3-5 team OKRs per quarter

### 7. **Sprint Plan Builder (Agile Iteration Planning)**
   - **Purpose:** Build actionable sprint plans with scope selection and velocity tracking
   - **Use When:** Sprint planning meetings, story estimation, workload balancing
   - **Key Tasks:**
     - Historical velocity calculation
     - Scope selection (based on team capacity)
     - Story breakdown into tasks (estimated effort)
     - Dependency analysis
     - Workload balancing across team
   - **Outputs:** Sprint backlog with priorities, task assignments, capacity view

### 8. **Agile Product Owner (INVEST-Compliant Backlog)**
   - **Purpose:** Manage backlog with INVEST criteria, sprint planning, and velocity tracking
   - **Use When:** Backlog refinement, sprint ceremonies, team communication, velocity trending
   - **Key Tasks:**
     - User story generation (INVEST-compliant: Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable)
     - Backlog prioritization (MoSCoW, value/effort matrix)
     - Sprint planning & velocity tracking
     - Stakeholder communication

## Product Management Frameworks

### INVEST User Story Criteria
- **I**ndependent: Minimal dependencies
- **N**egotiable: Details discussed with team
- **V**aluable: Delivers clear user value
- **E**stimable: Can be sized/effort-predicted
- **S**mall: Completable within one sprint
- **T**estable: Clear acceptance criteria

### Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD)
- Focus: What job is the customer trying to accomplish?
- Outcomes: Desired functional, emotional, social outcomes
- Success Metrics: How do you measure if job is done well?

### MoSCoW Prioritization
- **Must Have:** Critical for launch, non-negotiable
- **Should Have:** High-value, attempt in timeline
- **Could Have:** Nice-to-have, low-priority
- **Won't Have:** Out of scope, future consideration

### Build-Measure-Learn Loop
1. **Build** → Create MVP or feature increment
2. **Measure** → Collect user data (usage, feedback, metrics)
3. **Learn** → Analyze and refine hypothesis
4. **Iterate** → Repeat with new learnings

## Product Management Workflows

### Feature Launch Checklist
- [ ] Market research & competitive analysis completed
- [ ] PRD written & approved by stakeholders
- [ ] Technical feasibility validated
- [ ] Design mocks created & reviewed
- [ ] Engineering story points estimated
- [ ] Sprint planned & team capacity confirmed
- [ ] Launch date & support plan ready
- [ ] Success metrics dashboard live
- [ ] Customer communication plan ready
- [ ] Post-launch retrospective scheduled

### Roadmap Planning Checklist
- [ ] Company OKRs defined (quarterly/yearly)
- [ ] Customer feedback synthesized (interviews, support tickets, surveys)
- [ ] Competitive landscape assessed
- [ ] Technical debt prioritized
- [ ] Team capacity estimated
- [ ] Themes identified (new features, performance, stability, experimentation)
- [ ] Roadmap drafted with quarters/milestones
- [ ] Stakeholder alignment + buy-in
- [ ] Communication plan (internal + external)
