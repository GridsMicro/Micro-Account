## Core Campaign Skills

### 1. **Campaign Brief Generator**
   - **Purpose:** Create comprehensive campaign plan from objective to content calendar
   - **Use When:** Planning product launch, lead-gen campaign, awareness push, market entry
   - **Outputs:**
     - Campaign objective & success metrics
     - Target audience persona & messaging
     - Channel strategy (paid, organic, PR, partner)
     - Weekly content calendar with dependencies
     - Asset requirements & deadlines
     - Budget & ROI projection
     - Launch day checklist

### 2. **Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategist**
   - **Purpose:** Plan market entry for new product/feature with timing, channels, messaging
   - **Use When:** Launching a new GWS package or professional service
   - **Key Components:**
     - Product positioning (vs. competitors)
     - Customer segmentation & targeting
     - Channel prioritization (direct, partner, organic, paid)
     - Pricing & packaging strategy
     - Launch timeline & milestones
     - Launch day operations (team roles, monitoring, escalation)

### 3. **Content Calendar Builder**
   - **Purpose:** Generate week-by-week content plan with asset dependencies
   - **Use When:** Planning campaign content across multiple channels
   - **Outputs:**
     - Content matrix (blog, social, email, webinar, video)
     - Publication schedule (timing, frequency, channels)
     - Asset dependencies (graphics, copy, approvals)
     - Team assignments & deadlines
     - Performance tracking plan

### 4. **Channel Strategy Planner**
   - **Purpose:** Select and optimize mix of marketing channels for campaign
   - **Use When:** Choosing where to spend marketing budget, optimizing channel mix
   - **Channels:**
     - **Paid:** Google Ads (Search, Display, YouTube), Meta Ads (Facebook, Instagram), LinkedIn
     - **Organic:** Blog, YouTube, SEO, community (forums, LinkedIn groups)
     - **Direct:** Email, SMS, in-app notifications
     - **Partner:** Channel partners, reseller networks, affiliate programs
     - **PR:** Press releases, media relations, industry publications
   - **Framework:** Match channel to audience stage (awareness → consideration → decision)

### 5. **Launch Day Playbook**
   - **Purpose:** De-risk launch with coordinated team execution
   - **Use When:** Actually launching campaign or product
   - **Checklist:**
     - Pre-launch validation (links work, forms submit, analytics tracking live)
     - Team roles & escalation (who does what, who handles issues)
     - Monitoring dashboard (traffic, conversions, errors)
     - Communication plan (announce to teams, customers, press)
     - Support readiness (customer questions, technical issues)
     - Launch time triggers (email blasts, social posts, ads go live)
     - Post-launch review (48-hour debrief, learnings capture)

### Campaign Stages & Messaging
| Stage | Goal | Channels | Message Tone |
|-------|------|----------|--------------|
| **Awareness** | Reach target audience | Paid ads, social, PR | Educational, curiosity |
| **Consideration** | Highlight value | Content, webinar, email | Problem-solution, case study |
| **Decision** | Drive action | Landing page, sales | Urgency, proof, offer |
| **Retention** | Keep engaged | Email, community | Loyalty, upsell |

### Content Pillars (Example for GWS resale & AI services)
1. **Education:** Thai tax compliance, workflow automation how-tos
2. **Social Proof:** Customer success stories, ROI calculations
3. **Differentiation:** Local reseller support vs. buying direct (localization, support)
4. **Urgency:** Tax deadline reminders, limited-time offers

## Campaign Templates

### SaaS Product Launch Campaign
**Duration:** 4-6 weeks  
**Phases:**
- Week 1: Teaser (email to list, social hint)
- Week 2: Early access (beta sign-up, webinar invite)
- Week 3: Launch announcement (press release, ads, email)
- Week 4: Social proof (customer reviews, case studies)
- Week 5-6: Conversion focus (limited-time offer, urgency, testimonials)

### B2B Lead Generation Campaign
**Duration:** 8-12 weeks  
**Phases:**
- Week 1-2: Build list (content marketing, ads, SEO)
- Week 3-4: Nurture (email sequences, webinar, resource guide)
- Week 5-6: Engage (sales outreach, demo offer, consultation)
- Week 7-12: Convert (negotiation, proposal, close)

### Partner Channel Campaign
**Duration:** Ongoing (quarterly refresh)  
**Components:**
- Partner enablement (training, resources, margin structure)
- Marketing development funds (co-op, co-branded content)
- Lead sharing (partner → company → partner referral)
- Joint campaigns (webinars, events, case studies)

## Campaign Success Metrics (KPIs)

**Awareness Stage**
- Impressions, reach, cost-per-impression (CPI)
- Video views, page visits, email open rate

**Consideration Stage**
- Click-through rate (CTR), cost-per-click (CPC)
- Time on page, scroll depth, content engagement

**Decision Stage**
- Conversion rate (CR), cost-per-acquisition (CPA)
- Demo requests, sales-qualified leads (SQLs)

**Retention Stage**
- Customer retention rate, NPS (Net Promoter Score)
- Upsell rate, customer lifetime value (LTV)

**Overall Campaign**
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)
- Marketing qualified leads (MQLs), sales qualified leads (SQLs)
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC), CAC payback period
